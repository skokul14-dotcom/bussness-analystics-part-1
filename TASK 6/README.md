# Week 6 – Business Trend Analysis using Area Chart in Tableau

## Objective

Analyze business performance over time using an Area Chart in Tableau and identify important trends and changes that can support business decision-making.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

## Fields Used

- **Time-based field:** Order Date
- **Business measure:** Sales

## Visualizations Created

1. **Monthly Sales Trend** – Area Chart
2. **Sales by Category** – Bar Chart
3. **Sales by Region** – Bar Chart
4. **Monthly Profit Trend** – Line Chart

## Key Business Insights

1. Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, showing changes in business performance.
2. The Area Chart helps identify periods of high and low sales and provides a clear view of the overall sales trend.
3. Different product categories contribute differently to total sales, helping identify stronger-performing categories.
4. Sales performance varies across regions, with some regions contributing more significantly to overall sales.
5. The Monthly Profit Trend helps identify changes in profitability throughout the year.

## Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides a clear understanding of business performance over time. The Area Chart highlights monthly sales trends and fluctuations, while the additional visualizations provide insights into category, regional, and profit performance. These insights can help the business improve sales planning, inventory management, profitability, and decision-making.
