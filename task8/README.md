# Week 8 – Business Category Analysis using Tree Map in Tableau

## Objective

Analyze the contribution of different business categories using a Tree Map in Tableau and identify categories and sub-categories that have the greatest impact on overall sales performance.

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information about:
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Sales
- Customer Name
- Region
- Segment
- Order Date
- Product Name
- Product ID
- Order ID

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Tree Map – Sales Contribution by Category & Sub-Category

The Tree Map represents sales contribution across different categories and sub-categories. The size of each block represents sales, while colors represent business categories.

### 2. Scatter Plot – Sales vs Customer Count by Sub-Category

The Scatter Plot compares sales with the number of unique customers for each sub-category. It helps identify sub-categories with high sales and strong customer reach.

### 3. Packed Bubble Chart – Sub-Category Sales Performance

The Packed Bubble Chart displays the sales performance of individual sub-categories. Larger bubbles indicate higher sales contribution.

### 4. Stacked Bar Chart – Regional Sales Contribution by Category

The Stacked Bar Chart compares sales across different regions and categories. It helps identify regional differences in category performance.

## Dashboard

The four visualizations were combined into a single interactive dashboard titled:

**Business Category Analysis Dashboard**

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of:
- Category-wise sales contribution
- Sub-category performance
- Customer reach
- Regional sales performance

## Business Insights

1. Technology-related sub-categories contribute significantly to overall sales, making them important contributors to business performance.

2. Furniture sub-categories also make a considerable contribution to sales, indicating consistent demand for furniture products.

3. Sales and customer count vary across sub-categories, showing that some products generate high sales from a smaller customer base while others have wider customer reach.

4. East and West regions show strong overall sales performance, indicating greater business contribution from these regions.

5. Category performance differs across regions, suggesting that customer preferences and product demand vary geographically.

## Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing sub-categories by maintaining adequate inventory and promoting products with strong sales performance.

2. Develop region-specific marketing strategies for lower-performing categories to increase customer engagement and improve sales.
